## Alias identity and compatibility

Schema v5 gains optional reviewed model_resolutions with requested/resolved
names, configured default effort and local provenance. Current mappings bind to
the control fingerprint and approval; version/default/provider changes or loss
of known mappings require review of the affected host entry. Reordering mappings
and allowed settings does not. Existing v5 packets without mappings retain their
old fingerprints and cannot detect unreported alias changes. Their presence is
not inferred or fabricated on upgrade.

Resolution is configuration evidence, not execution observation. Mapping details
stay in local receipts and do not enter Jev requests. Feedback refuses a receipt
from another control mapping. Known Fable mappings also retain the existing
Claude-worker exclusion. Existing role defaults/locks, other hosts and project
overrides are preserved by setup. Dynamic approval needs no study. Advanced
qualification must still be reviewed when the model or default effort changes;
old model evidence does not qualify a new alias target.
